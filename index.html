🖼️ Your own canvas

Upload an image or type text — we trace it into pixel art, you position it on the board, then pay per pixel to place it (0.1 GNOT/pixel for open space, free if it happens to match the official design).

Left-click (or click-and-drag) paints with the left color below; right-click paints with the right color -- pick 🧹 on either to make it erase instead.

Type whatever you want to spell out — rendered with a real font, then traced into pixel art the same way an uploaded image is, so you can size it as compact as you like. Press Enter for multiple lines.

Upload an image — it's traced into pixel art using the board's own 9-color palette, right here in your browser (nothing is uploaded anywhere).

💡 Best results come from simple, low-detail images — logos, icons, flat-color illustrations. Photos and detailed art (gradients, shading, busy backgrounds) tend to look messy once squeezed down to a handful of cells and only 9 colors.

⚠️ Before you upload

The tracer only works well on images that are already simple and low-resolution — logos, icons, flat-color art. It also can't match colors it doesn't have: only the board's own 9-color palette is available, so anything outside that gets mapped to the closest color, which can come out wrong.

❌ Too detailed — traces as noise

✅ Simple and flat — traces cleanly

If your image doesn't fit that (photos, gradients, lots of detail), we recommend free draw instead — you get full control over exactly which of the 9 colors lands on each cell.

👾 GNO Pixels

A collaborative on-chain canvas for gno.land — every single pixel is a real, signed transaction on sapphire-1, permanently recorded by the pixels realm. No backend, no database — the board you see is read straight off the chain.

🏁
Official design

An owner-curated gno.land logo sits at the center of the board — placing toward it is always free. Help finish it from the sidebar's progress card.

🛡️
Anti-vandalism pricing

Painting open space costs a small fee. Overwriting an official-design cell with the wrong color costs 10x more — griefing the logo is deliberately expensive, matching its own color is always free.

🕵️
Right-click any cell

See exactly who placed it and at what block height — every pixel's provenance is public and on-chain, not just its color.

🏆
Leaderboard

Every successful placement counts toward a public top-contributors ranking — see it from the header.

🖼️
Your own canvas

Upload an image, type text, or free-draw your own design — combine them, position it anywhere, and submit it as a shared target the whole community can help fill in, paying per pixel as they go.

⛓️
Fully on-chain, always live

No polling gimmicks — the board, palette, stats, and leaderboard are all queried live from the realm every few seconds.

Built independently by the Gno.Observer project — not affiliated with the gno.land team.
